# Sri Lanka Market Context for a Multi-Vertical Marketplace Launch (2026)

Research date: 1 September 2026. All figures as-cited; unverifiable items flagged at the end.

---

## 1. Vehicle Market

**Import ban lifted in phases (Oct 2024 – Feb 2025).** After ~5 years of a forex-crisis import ban (in place since early 2020): buses/special-purpose vehicles from 1 Oct 2024; commercial/cargo vehicles from 1 Dec 2024; **private cars and motorcycles from 1 Feb 2025** ([Ceylon Daily News](https://ceylondailynews.lk/home/2025/01/28/vehicle-import-categories/), [EconomyNext](https://economynext.com/sri-lanka-to-lift-all-vehicle-import-restrictions-by-february-2025-minister-179540/), [Provide Cars](https://providecars.co.jp/sri-lanka-resumes-private-car-and-motorcycle-imports-after-nearly-five-years/)).

**Tax regime is punitive:** excise duties of **200–300%** by engine capacity/motor power, **18% VAT**, a **50% surcharge on customs import duty** for personal vehicles from 1 Feb 2025, plus a luxury tax of **LKR 6–60 million per car**; importers must sell within 3 months or pay a 3% fee ([Just Auto](https://www.just-auto.com/news/sri-lanka-lifts-ban-on-vehicle-imports/), [NewsOnAir](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/sri-lanka-eases-car-import-restrictions-imposes-new-taxes-surcharge)). Taxes were expected to raise landed prices ~20%, adding LKR 500,000–1,000,000 per vehicle ([NewsOnAir](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/sri-lankas-revised-vehicle-import-taxes-set-to-raise-prices-by-20-starting-february)).

**Volume boom:** by 14 Oct 2025 Sri Lanka had imported **220,000+ vehicles** (55,447 cars; 142,524 motorcycles; 15,035 three-wheelers; 7,331 goods vehicles; 1,679 buses/vans) ([Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/2025/11/03/how-many-vehicles-has-sri-lanka-imported-so-far-in-2025/)). Full-year 2025 vehicle-import tax revenue: **Rs 896–904 billion**, more than double the Rs 441 bn projection ([EconomyNext](https://economynext.com/sri-lanka-earns-rs904bn-from-vehicle-imports-in-2025-258285/), [Newswire/CCC report](https://www.newswire.lk/2026/08/05/ccc-report-sri-lanka-vehicle-imports-generate-rs-896b-in-taxes/)). 327,000+ new vehicle registrations in H1 2026 ([Lanka News Web](https://lankanewsweb.net/archives/251850/motor-vehicle-import-revival-drives-record-revenue-economic-questions/)). A 2026 revenue/import-momentum slowdown is being flagged ([Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/251221/business-times/sri-lankas-vehicle-import-boom-faces-2026-revenue-shock-625027.html)).

**Used-car prices did NOT crash.** A ~5–10% dip on rumors in late 2024, then prices held or rose because heavily-taxed new imports set a high ceiling — e.g., Honda Vezel 2025-model cohort averages **Rs 19.27M**, 2026 arrivals **Rs 19.51M** ([PriceMart analysis](https://pricemart.lk/blog/sri-lanka-used-car-market-2026-price-data), [Fact Crescendo](https://srilanka.factcrescendo.com/english/are-vehicle-prices-dropping-significantly-in-sri-lanka/), [CAL](https://cal.lk/vehicle-import-ban-lifted-whats-next-for-car-prices-cal-sri-lanka/)). Reference point: a Toyota Aqua listed around **Rs 13.5M** (Aug 2025) ([riyasewana](https://riyasewana.com/search/cars/toyota/aqua), [patpat.lk](https://patpat.lk/en/sri-lanka/vehicle/car/toyota/aqua)).

**Leasing:** rates in 2025 ran ~**13–18% p.a.** for passenger vehicles (up to ~25% at some NBFIs), down payments typically **20–30%** ([findit.lk](https://www.findit.lk/recommendations/car-leasing-rates-in-sri-lanka), [IdeaBeam rate comparison](https://www.ideabeam.com/finance/vehicle-leasing.html)). Major players: **LOLC Finance** (largest NBFI after its Jan 2022 merger; 219 branches) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LOLC_Finance)), **People's Leasing & Finance** (est. 1995, 111 branches) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Leasing_%26_Finance)), plus bank leasing arms (BOC, Commercial Bank, Pan Asia) ([BOC](https://www.boc.lk/personal-banking/leasing/leasing/leasing), [Pan Asia](https://www.pabcbank.com/personal-banking/loans-leasing/pan-asia-bank-leasing/pan-asia-bank-normal-leasing/)). Implication: leasing-partner lead-gen is a proven monetization path for a vehicle vertical.

## 2. Property Market

- **Land prices:** Colombo District +11.4% YoY in H1 2025 (residential +14.4%); +7.7% in Q2 2025 alone ([Asia Property Awards](https://www.asiapropertyawards.com/en/sustaining-sri-lankas-real-estate-revival-colombos-growth-story-and-its-future/), [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/gxNMYvG1lCncDBPok5u9)).
- Market described as transitioning **from buyers' to sellers' market** ([Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Sri-Lanka-s-real-estate-market-transitioning-from-buyers-market-to-sellers-market/44-714911)); pre-2015 luxury apartments +48% over five years ([Akara](https://www.akaraapartments.lk/colombo-luxury-real-estate-trends-2025/)).
- **Rental yields:** ~3% city-centre / 3.6% outside (Numbeo) vs ~4–5% per Lanka Property Web ([The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/gxNMYvG1lCncDBPok5u9)). Colombo rents: budget apartments from ~Rs 360k–400k/month to Rs 1.1M–3M+ luxury; expat demand is propping up the top end ([LankaPropertyWeb](https://www.lankapropertyweb.com/rentals/lease-Colombo+All_0-Apartment.html), [Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/260405/business-times/expats-thrive-in-costly-colombo-rental-market-637274.html)).
- **Diaspora:** ~**20% of apartment sales** are diaspora buyers (80% local), concentrated in Colombo 6; locals favour Colombo 2 ([The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/gxNMYvG1lCncDBPok5u9)). Incumbent LankaPropertyWeb publishes an annual Market Outlook Report ([MOR26](https://www.lankapropertyweb.com/MOR26)).

## 3. Digital Economy

- **Internet:** 12.4M users, **53.6% penetration** (Jan 2025), +808k (+7.0%) YoY; 8.20M social media identities (**35.4%** of population); 29.3M mobile connections; Android ~84% vs iOS ~15% ([DataReportal Digital 2025: Sri Lanka](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-sri-lanka), [TRCSL Q1 2025](https://www.trc.gov.lk/content/files/statistics/TSR2025Q1TelecommunicationsStatisticalreport21052025331pmforupdateTRCSLweb.pdf)). Note: "80%+ smartphone ownership" claims circulate but are vendor estimates.
- **Payments:** LankaQR (CBSL standard, 2020) now covers **400,000+ merchants** via 22 acquirers and 30+ apps at 0.5–1% capped MDR; interbank transfers (CEFTS/RTGS) now ~**70% of retail transaction volume** ([Stanchion](https://www.stanchionpayments.com/sri-lanka-payments-evolution-consumer-payment-methods/)). Gateways/wallets: **PayHere** (dominant SME gateway), **Genie** (Dialog), **FriMi** (Nations Trust); BNPL via **Mintpay** and **Koko** ([Fintech Times](https://thefintechtimes.com/sri-lanka-can-digital-finance-help-rebuild-trust-in-the-economy/), [FinAP](https://finapworldwide.com/buy-now-pay-later-qr-payments-in-sri-lanka-bnpl/)).
- **Cards:** only ~**2.14M active credit cards** (end-Nov 2025, +6.8% YTD) — ~**5% credit-card penetration** despite 74% bank-account ownership; 14 banks + 4 finance companies licensed for card services ([EconomyNext](https://economynext.com/sri-lanka-sees-10785-new-active-credit-cards-in-november-2025-258089/), [CBSL Payments Bulletin Q3 2025](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/Payments_Bulletin_3Q2025_e.pdf), [Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/Columnists/Setting-sights-on-the-next-million-credit-card-users/4-687341)). Cash-on-delivery and bank transfer remain essential.
- **E-commerce:** B2C e-commerce projected at ~**US$2.65 bn in 2025**, heading to ~US$3.2 bn by 2029; online share of retail still low single digits (government cited ~2% in 2023 targeting 4–5% by 2025) ([ResearchAndMarkets/GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/29/3228355/28124/en/sri-lanka-b2c-ecommerce-report-2025-a-3-2-billion-market-by-2029-size-forecast-by-value-and-volume-across-80-kpis.html), [Daily News](https://dailynews.lk/2023/10/27/business/192542/e-commerce-usage-to-increase-to-5-from-current-2-by-2025/)). **Daraz** (Alibaba) is the retail e-commerce leader ([Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/Business/Trends-and-growth-of-Sri-Lanka-s-e-commerce-industry-An-overview-of-Daraz-e-commerce-Index/34-703498)); **Facebook Marketplace and Groups are a major informal C2C channel** across all age groups ([Hashmeta](https://hashmeta.com/blog/social-media-landscape-sri-lanka-complete-guide-to-platforms-trends-strategy/), [Adaderana](https://bizenglish.adaderana.lk/facebook-marketplace-comes-to-sri-lanka/)).

## 4. Trust Environment

- **Sri Lanka CERT|CC received 2,887 cybercrime complaints in Q1 2025 alone, 364 of them financial fraud**; CERT and the CID have issued repeated public warnings about social-media and WhatsApp scams ([The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/YxONErqJiOnmCaG945DH), [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/breaking-news/Rise-in-social-media-scams-targeting-Sri-Lankans-CID-warns-public/108-312761), [CERT](https://cert.gov.lk/)).
- Fake listings on **ikman.lk and Daraz** (pay-and-disappear sellers, fake jobs) are among the most-cited scam types ([Pal Horu](https://palhoru.com/blog/top-10-most-popular-scams-in-sri-lanka)); ikman itself runs safety-education campaigns ([ikman blog](https://blog.ikman.lk/en/stay-safe-online-with-ikman/)).
- Regulatory backdrop: the controversial **Online Safety Act No. 9 of 2024** (in force 1 Feb 2024) created an Online Safety Commission, but the current government paused enforcement pending amendments ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_Safety_Act_(Sri_Lanka)), [Sri Lanka Brief](https://srilankabrief.org/online-safety-act-enforcement-halted/)). Takeaway: verified-seller programs, escrow, and in-app safety tooling are genuine differentiators here.

## 5. Language

Sinhala ~74%, Tamil ~18%, English spoken by ~23.8% ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_Sri_Lanka)). All three are official; Sinhala/Tamil Unicode input and browsing are mainstream, with ICTA running a Local Languages Initiative ([ICTA](https://www.icta.lk/local-languages-initiative)). **ikman.lk supports all three languages** and was reportedly the first trilingual classifieds portal ([ikman](https://ikman.lk/en/ads), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikman.lk)) — trilingual UX is table stakes, not a differentiator, against ikman; most smaller rivals (riyasewana, patpat) are weaker on Tamil.

## 6. Competitors, Funding, Market Size

- **Saltside (ikman.lk):** raised **US$65M total** — $25M (2014, Kinnevik-backed) + $40M (Jan 2015, Hillhouse-led with Brummer & Partners, Kinnevik) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2014/06/19/with-25m-backing-saltside-wants-to-be-the-craigslist-for-emerging-markets/), [FinSMEs](https://www.finsmes.com/2015/01/saltside-technologies-raises-40m-in-funding.html)). It sold **Tonaton (Ghana) to Jiji in 2022** and **Bikroy (Bangladesh) to Jiji in May 2026**, leaving **ikman.lk as its main remaining asset** ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/05/12/jiji-acquires-bikroy-bangladesh-startup/), [Future Startup](https://futurestartup.com/2026/05/14/jiji-acquires-bikroy-jijis-motivation-bikroys-reason-and-what-it-tells-about-the-bangladesh-market/)). ikman: ~**5.92M monthly visits** (Mar 2025), 575k+ active ads ([ikman blog](https://blog.ikman.lk/en/ikman-lk-leads-sri-lankan-retail-sites-e-commerce-brand-rankings/), [Saltside](https://saltside.se/)).
- **Strategic watch item:** Jiji's playbook is "enter organically, then buy the leader" — a **jiji.lk** classifieds site with Sri Lanka listings is already live ([jiji.lk](https://jiji.lk/)), suggesting ikman could be Jiji's next target/competitor. No press announcement of a formal jiji.lk Sri Lanka launch was found — treat as observed but unconfirmed.
- **Vehicle vertical incumbents:** riyasewana.com (since 2009; ~1.5M monthly users, ~3.2M pageviews/month per third-party estimates) and patpat.lk (~22k live vehicle listings) ([statshow](https://www.statshow.com/www/riyasewana.com), [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/patpat.lk/)). Property incumbent: LankaPropertyWeb (8,200+ rental listings live).
- **Ad market sizing:** Sri Lanka online advertising market ~**US$385M (2025)** → US$532M by 2032 (5.5% CAGR) ([6Wresearch](https://www.6wresearch.com/industry-report/sri-lanka-online-advertising-market)); Statista tracks digital advertising separately ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/outlook/dmo/digital-advertising/sri-lanka)). No credible standalone "Sri Lanka classifieds market" figure exists.

## Could not verify / conflicting data

1. **No public revenue figures for ikman.lk or PatPat** — Saltside revenue estimates conflict wildly ($1.7M vs $126M from data-scraper sites); neither is trustworthy. Saltside is private and Swedish filings weren't accessible.
2. **Classifieds-specific market size for Sri Lanka** — only broad online-ad ($385M) and e-commerce GMV proxies exist; a Quora "$5.4bn" figure is not credible.
3. **Social-commerce share of e-commerce** — Facebook Groups/Marketplace dominance is well documented qualitatively, but no reliable percentage of transactions was found.
4. **Smartphone penetration "80%+ / 120%"** — connection counts exceed population (~29.3M connections) but actual smartphone-ownership rates come from vendor blogs, not official statistics; DataReportal's 53.6% internet penetration is the safer planning number.
5. **2026 rental price growth** — CPI shows housing driving non-food inflation (7.8% non-food, May 2026) but no clean Colombo rent index exists.

**Key implications for a 2026 launch:** vehicles is the hottest vertical (220k+ imports, high-ticket, leasing lead-gen monetization); trust/escrow is the clearest gap vs ikman; trilingual support is mandatory; payments must lean on LankaQR/bank transfer/COD, not cards; and watch Jiji's Sri Lanka entry, which may reshape the competitive map mid-launch.
