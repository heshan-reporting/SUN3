# Marketplace Platform Research: Features & Revenue Models Worth Mimicking for a Sri Lankan Multi-Vertical Platform

Research date: September 2026. Covers vehicles, vehicle rentals, property, home & living/electronics, plus a Sri Lanka-specific synthesis.

---

## PART 1: VEHICLES

### 1.1 Carvana (US — full-stack online retailer)

**Killer features**
1. **End-to-end online purchase** — browse, finance, sign, and schedule delivery entirely online; car vending towers as marketing theater.
2. **7-day money-back return window** — functions as a *revenue driver*, not a cost center: it collapses purchase anxiety, and since few buyers actually return, incremental sales far exceed return-processing costs.
3. **In-house financing (Carvana Auto Finance)** — ~62% financing attach rate (Q1 2024); loans are securitized/sold with gain-on-sale booked in high-margin "other revenue."
4. **Attach products**: Vehicle Service Contracts (extended warranty), GAP waivers, insurance.
5. **Instant trade-in offers** — sourcing inventory directly from consumers cheaply.

**Revenue model**: retail vehicle margin (bought → reconditioned → sold at markup; 416,348 retail units, $9.68B retail revenue in 2024), wholesale of non-retail-grade cars, and financing/attach products (the profit engine — the "other" line is nearly pure margin).

**Trust & safety**: 150-point inspection, no-haggle fixed pricing, 7-day return, free Carfax history.

Sources: [Carvana IR](https://investors.carvana.com/~/media/Files/C/Carvana-IR/documents/investor-relations-benefits-of-carvanas-differentiated-business-model-september-2025.pdf), [How Carvana Makes Money](https://www.thevantagegroupauto.com/blog/how-carvana-makes-money), [Economy Insights](https://www.economyinsights.com/p/how-carvana-makes-money), [Umbrex profile](https://umbrex.com/resources/company-profiles/carvana/)

### 1.2 CarGurus (US — lead-gen/subscription classifieds)

**Killer features**
1. **Instant Market Value (IMV)** — algorithmic fair-value price for every listing.
2. **Deal ratings** ("Great/Good/Fair/Overpriced" badges) — turns price transparency into the default sort, forcing dealers to price honestly to get ranked.
3. **Dealer reviews/ratings** surfaced beside listings.
4. **Digital Deal** — buyers complete paperwork, financing pre-qualification, and delivery arrangement online.

**Revenue model**: ~87% of revenue from **dealer subscriptions** (monthly, priced by inventory volume, region, and estimated ROI — US quarterly average revenue per subscribing dealer approaches ~$8,000, i.e., ~$2.5k+/month); remainder from OEM display advertising (CPM) and add-on products. Consumers pay nothing.

**Trust & safety**: algorithmic price policing (a listing's rank suffers if overpriced), dealer rating system.

Sources: [Umbrex — CarGurus](https://umbrex.com/resources/company-profiles/cargurus/), [LSE IRG analysis](https://medium.com/lse-irg/cargurus-a-high-quality-business-414d628caf22), [CarGurus dealer pricing policy](https://www.cargurus.com/about/dealer-pricing-policy)

### 1.3 Auto Trader UK (the profitability gold standard)

**Killer features**
1. **Near-monopoly audience** → dealers *must* be present (14,013 retailers).
2. **Tiered advertising packages** ("the more you invest, the more response you get") with structural annual price/product increases.
3. **Dealer tooling**: valuations, stock management, performance analytics bundled into packages.

**Revenue model**: dealer subscription/advertising packages. **ARPR (average revenue per retailer) = £2,854/month** (FY25, +5% YoY), grown deliberately via three levers — price, product (upsell; 33% of stock on above-standard packages), stock. Operating margin ~70%, £377M profit. This is the classifieds end-state to aspire to.

Sources: [Auto Trader FY25 results](https://plc.autotrader.co.uk/media/umddcnxx/full-year-press-release-fy25.pdf), [AM Online](https://www.am-online.com/news/auto-trader-profit-rises-to-377m-as-dealer-demand-fuels-platform-growth), [Auto Trader retailer packages](https://www.autotrader.co.uk/partners/retailer/packages)

### 1.4 Cars24 & Spinny (India — full-stack refurb-and-certify)

**Killer features**
- **Spinny**: 200-point inspection with refusal to list failures (the foundational trust decision), certified + warrantied inventory, **5-day money-back return** (first in India), fixed prices, home test drives, D2C full-stack control.
- **Cars24**: 300-point quality check, broader platform (buy/sell/finance/scrappage/paperwork/challan services), instant sell-your-car offers with same-day payment and free RC transfer — solving the *seller* pain point of paperwork in a bureaucratic market.

**Revenue model**: margin between buy and sell price on refurbished cars + financing (both run NBFC lending arms in India) + value-added services (warranty, insurance, paperwork). Spinny revenue ₹590Cr (FY22) → ₹1,680Cr (FY24), losses narrowing; Cars24 operates at ~₹4,600Cr+ scale. Note: both are capital-intensive and loss-making for years — a caution for a Sri Lankan clone.

**Trust & safety**: inspection certification, warranty, return window, assured RC/title transfer handled by the platform.

Sources: [StartupTalky — Spinny business model](https://startuptalky.com/spinny-business-model/), [Cars24 vs Spinny comparison](https://cararth.com/throttle-talk/guide/cars24-vs-spinny), [TFIPost analysis](https://tfipost.com/2026/04/cars24-vs-spinny-decoding-business-model-and-how-it-impacts-used-car-buyers-in-india/)

### 1.5 Carousell (Southeast Asia — horizontal C2C gone vertical)

**Killer features**
1. **Mobile-first, 30-second "snap-to-sell" listing** — lowest possible listing friction.
2. **Bumps** (paid re-ranking) as micro-monetization anyone can afford.
3. **Carousell Certified** (cars, luxury, electronics): in-house inspection + warranty + authorized partners; certified items show **~40% higher sell-through** and by 2025 contribute ~15% of revenue.
4. **CarouBiz** merchant subscription (quotas, analytics, badges).

**Revenue model (approximate mix)**: ads/visibility + bumps ~40%, merchant subscriptions ~15%, Certified ~15%, remainder from payments/shipping and vertical services. Evolution path: free classifieds → paid visibility → subscriptions → certified/managed transactions.

**Trust & safety**: user ratings, in-app chat, escrow-backed "Buyer Protection" on Carousell-paid transactions, Certified programs for high-value categories.

Sources: [ProductMint — Carousell](https://productmint.com/carousell-business-model-how-does-carousell-make-money/), [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/how-does-carousell-make-money/), [BusinessModelHub](https://businessmodelhub.in/carousell-business-model/)

### 1.6 Dubizzle / dubicars (UAE)

**Killer features**: free basic listings to keep liquidity; a dedicated **dealer app (dubizzle motors)** with inventory management; premium placement products; expansion into adjacent services (inspections, insurance, mortgages).

**Revenue model**: featured/premium listings, **dealer packages ~AED 199–999/month** for listing slots, display advertising, lead/data sales to businesses, and new verticals (certified inspections, insurance and mortgage brokerage referral revenue).

**Trust & safety**: verified-dealer badges, certified inspection add-ons.

Sources: [BusinessModelHub — Dubizzle](https://businessmodelhub.in/dubizzle-business-model/), [Dubizzle motors dealer app launch](https://www.eyeofriyadh.com/news/details/dubizzle-launches-a-new-dedicated-app-for-uae-car-dealers), [Vizologi](https://vizologi.com/business-strategy-canvas/dubizzle-business-model-canvas/)

### 1.7 Encar (South Korea, owned by CAR Group) + Chileautos

**Killer features**
1. **Encar Diagnosis / Guarantee Inspection** — platform-operated physical inspection centers (62 centers, penetration reached ~59% of new listings) producing standardized condition reports; the inspection is a *paid product with warranty backing*, not just a badge.
2. **Mobile Diagnosis AI** — auto-extracts plate, mileage, model, and options from photos to standardize listings and catch misrepresentation.
3. Massive liquidity: ~40% of all Korean used-car registrations touch Encar; export service for international buyers.
4. Detailed media-rich listings (photo/video walkarounds) as market norm.

**Revenue model** (CAR Group playbook, also applied at Chileautos and Webmotors): dealer listing subscriptions + premium placement + **paid guarantee inspections** + finance/insurance lead referrals + data/audience products sold to dealers, OEMs, insurers; consistent annual price increases with negligible churn.

**Trust & safety**: mandatory-ish inspection culture (buyers filter for inspected cars), accident-history checks, platform warranty on inspected vehicles.

Sources: [AIM Group — Encar 40% share](https://aimgroup.com/2025/03/11/encar-accounts-for-40-of-used-car-registration-in-south-korea/), [Encar diagnosis center](https://newautopost.co.kr/en/auto-newsroom/article/115960/), [CAR Group — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAR_Group), [Mopoke Cloud CAR Group deep dive](https://www.mopokecloud.com/p/deep-dive-car-group-carsalescomau)

## PART 2: VEHICLE RENTALS

### 2.1 Turo (P2P car sharing — the survivor)

**Killer features**
1. **Host protection/earnings plans**: hosts pick their commission tier — keep **70/80/90%** depending on how much damage protection Turo provides (higher protection = higher take for Turo). Every plan bundles third-party liability insurance (via Travelers in the US) + physical damage reimbursement + roadside assistance.
2. Dynamic pricing tools for hosts; guest trip fees (2.5%+ of rental, disclosed upfront).
3. Deep screening of guests (license verification, risk scoring).

**Revenue model**: commission spread (10–35% host fee by plan) + guest trip fees + young-driver fees + delivery/extras. The insurance tiering is the elegant part: Turo monetizes risk appetite in both directions.

**Trust & safety**: identity/license verification, pre/post-trip photo documentation, embedded insurance, two-way reviews, claims handling.

Sources: [Sacra — Turo](https://sacra.com/c/turo/), [Turo insurance page](https://turo.com/us/en/car-rental/united-states/insurance), [Oyelabs — Turo revenue model](https://oyelabs.com/turo-revenue-model-explained/)

### 2.2 Getaround (cautionary tale)

**Model**: instant, keyless rentals via proprietary **Connect hardware** ($99 install + ~$20/month per car), **40% commission** on hosts (covering insurance, roadside, support), 15–30% renter service fees. **Outcome: shut down all US operations in Feb 2025 after running out of liquidity**; only its European business survives. Lessons: hardware-heavy instant access + very high take rate + thin margins on insurance-heavy short rentals did not work; Turo's simpler booking-based model with tiered insurance did.

Sources: [TechCrunch — Getaround shuts US ops](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/12/getaround-abruptly-shuts-down-us-car-sharing-operations/), [Getaround wind-down release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/02/12/3024676/0/en/Getaround-Inc-Announces-Wind-Down-of-U-S-Operations.html), [Leli — Getaround review](https://www.leli.rentals/review/getaround)

**Local rent-a-car implication**: in markets like Sri Lanka, rentals are dominated by small fleet operators (often with-driver). The winning marketplace model is closer to "Turo-for-fleets": listings + calendar/booking + deposit escrow + insurance verification, monetized by booking commission (10–20%) or operator subscription — not P2P hardware.

## PART 3: PROPERTY

### 3.1 Zillow (US)

**Killer features**
1. **Zestimate** — free algorithmic valuation on *every* home (not just listings) — the consumer traffic magnet and habit-former.
2. **Premier Agent** — sells buyer leads/adjacent ad placement on listings to agents (fixed monthly spend by ZIP code share-of-voice).
3. **Flex** — pay-on-success referral: agent pays **25–40% of gross commission** only when the deal closes (recently raised to 40% in some markets). This is the model that works when agents can't/won't pay upfront.
4. Mortgage/rentals adjacencies.

**Trust & safety**: verified listings via MLS feeds, agent profiles with reviews and sales history.

Sources: [Mike DelPrete — Flex fee to 40%](https://www.mikedp.com/articles/2023/10/12/zillow-flex-fee-rises-to-40-percent), [The Close — Zillow Flex](https://theclose.com/zillow-flex/), [Ylopo — Zillow lead gen](https://www.ylopo.com/blog/zillow-lead-generation)

### 3.2 Rightmove (UK — the SaaS-not-classifieds model)

**Killer features**: absolute audience dominance; **agents pay a fixed monthly membership** (not per-listing, not per-lead) with tiered packages (Essential → Enhanced → Optimiser → Optimiser Edge); relentless product-led ARPA growth.

**Revenue model**: **ARPA £1,524/month** (2024, +£93 YoY); agency ARPA £1,440, new-homes developer ARPA £1,987. Over 36% of agents on top packages; 1,600+ upgraded to Optimiser Edge in its first year. ~70%+ operating margins. Key insight: sell *membership to the audience* as B2B SaaS — predictable recurring revenue, upgrade ladder, price rises annually.

Sources: [Rightmove FY results RNS](https://plc.rightmove.co.uk/content/uploads/2024/02/RNS-2023-29-Feb-FINAL-signed.pdf), [Property Industry Eye](https://propertyindustryeye.com/eye-newsflash-rightmove-post-strong-full-year-results/), [Letting Agent Today](https://www.lettingagenttoday.co.uk/breaking-news/2025/03/rising-agency-revenues-boost-rightmove-profits/)

### 3.3 PropertyGuru (SEA)

**Model**: Rightmove-style agent subscriptions adapted to SEA — tiered annual agent packages + "depth" credits (boost/feature individual listings), developer advertising, plus **PropertyGuru Finance** (mortgage brokerage; facilitated SGD 6B+ in home loans by end-2024) and data/valuation products. In mature markets (~14,000 Singapore agents) growth comes from ARPA, not agent count. ~SGD 190M FY2025 revenue, ~75% from subscriptions + digital advertising.

Sources: [POEMS — PropertyGuru](https://www.poems.com.sg/stocks/articles/propertyguru-poised-to-take-its-plans-forward/), [PropertyGuru press release](https://www.propertyguru.com.sg/customer-service/press-releases/propertyguru-group-delivers-41-increase-in-revenue), [Online Marketplaces analysis](https://www.onlinemarketplaces.com/articles/analysis-as-it-prepares-for-public-debut-is-propertyguru/)

### 3.4 MagicBricks / 99acres (India)

**Model**: lead-gen and visibility, monetizing *both sides*: owners pay to list/unlock buyer contacts; brokers/builders buy subscription packages. 99acres: owner premium plans from ~₹899/month; broker "BOSS" packages from ~₹3,149/month with real spends ₹4,000–60,000/month by city; overall ₹6,000–25,000/year typical. MagicBricks similar, bundling listings with paid visibility boosts and monetizing access to owner contact details. Builders pay for featured projects, banners, microsites. Notable Indian counter-model: **NoBroker** charges buyers/tenants to bypass brokers entirely — evidence that "broker-avoidance" itself is monetizable in South Asia.

Sources: [StartupTalky — MagicBricks business model](https://startuptalky.com/magicbricks-business-model/), [ClosingFox comparison](https://closingfox.com/for-business-owners/99acres-vs-magicbricks-vs-housing/), [Freeperty comparison](https://blog.freeperty.com/free-property-listing-vs-99acres-vs-magicbricks-which-platfo/)

### 3.5 Airbnb (short stays)

**Model**: transaction take rate. As of late 2025, Airbnb moved everyone to a **single host-only fee of 15.5%** of the booking subtotal (replacing 3% host + ~14% guest split); 16% in Brazil/Mexico. Killer features: escrow payments (guest pays Airbnb, host paid ~24h after check-in), two-way reviews, AirCover damage protection, identity verification, instant book. The lesson: full payment intermediation is what makes a ~15% take rate defensible.

Sources: [Houst — Airbnb fees](https://www.houst.com/blog/airbnb-hosting-fees), [Hostfully — host fees](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/airbnb-host-fees/), [RentalScaleUp](https://www.rentalscaleup.com/airbnb-host-fees/)

## PART 4: HOME & LIVING / ELECTRONICS

### 4.1 Wayfair

**Model**: drop-ship marketplace (20,000+ suppliers, no inventory ownership for most SKUs) + **CastleGate** owned logistics for fast-movers + supplier advertising fees + Wayfair Rewards membership. Killer features: enormous curated catalog with rich visualization (AR, 3D), financing options, white-glove delivery. Caution: shipping bulky goods ate >25% of revenue in some years — logistics economics are brutal in furniture.

Sources: [Latterly — Wayfair business model](https://www.latterly.org/wayfair-business-model/), [Think Insights](https://thinkinsights.net/strategy/wayfair-marketplace-business-model), [Supply Chain Dive](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/wayfair-logistics-investment/573199/)

### 4.2 Urban Ladder (India — cautionary/curation lesson)

Curated **private-label** furniture with in-house design, quality control and omnichannel showrooms rather than an open marketplace. Great brand recall, but capital-heavy; eventually sold to Reliance (96% stake, 2020) after failing to reach sustainable scale. Lesson: in furniture, curation and quality control build trust, but pure D2C private label is a retail business, not a marketplace.

Sources: [Inc42 — rise and fall](https://inc42.com/features/urbanladder-reliance-furniture-market/), [IIDE case study](https://iide.co/case-studies/business-model-of-urban-ladder/)

### 4.3 Back Market (refurbished electronics)

**Killer features**
1. **Standardized condition grading** (Fair/Good/Excellent) — replaces seller-written condition claims with platform vocabulary.
2. **Mandatory 12-month warranty + 30-day money-back** on every listing — neutralizes the core fear of used electronics.
3. Curated/vetted professional refurbishers only (quality-scored, mystery-purchased) — not open C2C.
4. "Best offer wins the buy box" per grade — sellers compete on price/quality score.

**Revenue model**: ~10% seller commission + ~€50/month seller fee + ~2% buyer service fee (+€0.49); blended **take rate ~12.6–14.8%**; 2025: €440M revenue on €3.5B GMV. Mix: ~68% seller commission, 14% consumer fees, 18% fast-growing add-ons (insurance, extended warranty, seller ads, carrier deals).

Sources: [Sacra — Back Market](https://sacra.com/c/back-market/), [Global Sources — seller guide](https://www.globalsources.com/knowledge/how-to-become-a-seller-on-backmarket/), [TheBrandHopper](https://thebrandhopper.com/featured-startups/back-market-founders-business-model-revenue-streams-funding/)

### 4.4 OLX / Avito (horizontal classifieds)

**Model**: free listings for liquidity → monetize via (1) featured/spotlight/urgent paid promotions, (2) business-seller subscription packages, (3) display advertising, (4) value-added transaction services (secure payment, shipping) in selected markets. **Avito (Russia)** is the world's most profitable horizontal classifieds — heavy paid-placement + per-category listing limits that force professional sellers to pay. **Strategic arc**: OLX built a transactional used-car arm (OLX Autos), lost money, and exited it entirely by Aug 2025, pivoting back to high-margin classifieds focused on **jobs, motors, real estate** with "outcome/transaction-led" monetization rather than pure visibility. The fraud-detection and verification tech from Autos was retained platform-wide.

Sources: [FourWeekMBA — OLX](https://fourweekmba.com/how-does-olx-make-money-olx-business-model/), [Online Marketplaces — OLX B2C shift](https://www.onlinemarketplaces.com/articles/olx-signals-shift-of-strategy-towards-b2c-at-capital-markets-day/), [The Arc — OLX exits auto](https://www.thearcweb.com/article/olx-shuts-auto-classifieds-business-in-india-udZAGK9XmJWLZvHG)

### 4.5 Mercari / Vinted (escrow + shipping)

**Mercari**: flat **10% seller commission**; platform-generated **prepaid shipping labels** (partner rates up to 54% below retail — a margin and convenience product); **full escrow** — buyer pays Mercari, funds released to seller only after buyer confirms receipt (or 3 days post-delivery); optional paid authentication for luxury goods.

**Vinted**: **sellers pay zero** (liquidity flywheel); buyers pay a **"Buyer Protection" fee (~5% + €0.70 fixed)** that funds the escrow + refund guarantee; margin on integrated shipping (Vinted Go locker network, 7,000+ points); in-house payment processing (Vinted Pay EMI). Escrow releases 2 days after delivery if no complaint. This is the cleanest proof that *buyers in scam-prone categories will pay for trust* — the trust layer IS the revenue model.

Sources: [Sacra — Vinted](https://sacra.com/c/vinted/), [Revenue Memo — Vinted](https://www.revenuememo.com/p/how-does-vinted-make-money), [Crosslist — Mercari fees](https://crosslist.com/blog/mercari-seller-fees), [Sharetribe — Mercari guide](https://www.sharetribe.com/create/how-to-build-website-like-mercari/)

### 4.6 Facebook Marketplace

1B+ users; free listings, monetized lightly via **boosted listings** and business ads; minimal verification, no escrow for local deals → scam-heavy (ProPublica documented systemic fraud-moderation failures). It is simultaneously your biggest competitor for liquidity and your best marketing argument: *"the safe alternative to Facebook Marketplace"* is a viable positioning in any scam-anxious market.

Sources: [ProPublica investigation](https://www.propublica.org/article/facebook-grew-marketplace-to-1-billion-users-now-scammers-are-using-it-to-target-people-around-the-world), [TechCrunch — Marketplace ads](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1652583), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/scammers-turn-to-facebook-marketplace-as-it-hits-a-billion-users)

### 4.7 Jiji (Africa — emerging-market playbook)

Pan-African classifieds leader (12–15M MAU across 7+ countries, 3M+ listings). Deliberately **free-to-list because upfront-fee tolerance is low in low-income markets**; monetizes sellers who *are* making money via premium/boost packages tiered by duration and category; local teams and phone-based support per country; acquired Cars45 to add inspections/transactions selectively. Growth engine was aggressive SEO + lightweight app for low-end Android + agents onboarding informal sellers.

Sources: [ProductMint — Jiji](https://productmint.com/how-does-jiji-make-money/), [Afrobility podcast analysis](https://afrobility.com/episode/51-jiji-how-the-classifieds-platform-became-the-largest-marketplace-in-west-east-africa), [TechCrunch — Jiji raises $21M](https://techcrunch.com/2019/12/09/jiji-raises-21m-for-its-africa-online-classifieds-business)

## PART 5: SYNTHESIS — WHAT TRANSLATES TO SRI LANKA

### 5.1 Market context (2025–26)

- **Vehicle imports resumed Feb 2025** after a ~5-year ban, but with 200–300% excise duties — so used vehicles remain extremely expensive, price-opaque, and highly financialized assets; the existing fleet is old and inspection matters enormously. Prices per model-year kept rising even after the ban lifted. ([CAL](https://cal.lk/vehicle-import-ban-lifted-whats-next-for-car-prices-cal-sri-lanka/), [Provide Cars](https://providecars.co.jp/sri-lanka-resumes-private-car-and-motorcycle-imports-after-nearly-five-years/), [PriceMart](https://pricemart.lk/blog/sri-lanka-used-car-market-2026-price-data))
- **Incumbents**: ikman.lk (horizontal, Saltside) and Riyasewana (vehicles) — classic free-listing + paid-promotion classifieds. Riyasewana already integrates a **DMT (Dept. of Motor Traffic) API for instant title checks** — a strong local trust precedent. Organized used-car providers growing ~9.8% CAGR on the back of inspection centers, escrow payments, and powertrain warranties. ([Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/sri-lanka-used-car-market), [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/sri-lanka-used-car-market))
- **Payments/mobile**: mobile penetration >100%, ~72% access internet via smartphone (rural leading), LankaQR/CEFTS instant transfers now ~70% of retail transaction volume, GovPay launched 2025 — meaning **digital escrow is now technically feasible** even in a cash-default culture. ([CBSL](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/en/news/cbsl-launches-the-digital-payments-promotion-campaign-2025), [Stanchion](https://www.stanchionpayments.com/sri-lanka-payments-evolution-consumer-payment-methods/), [Innovest](https://innovestinvestments.com/digital-sri-lanka-payments-governance-e%E2%80%91infrastructure/))

### 5.2 What emerging-market players did differently from Western players

| Western pattern | Emerging-market adaptation (OLX, Carousell, Jiji, Cars24) |
|---|---|
| Charge listers upfront (Rightmove/AutoTrader) | **Free listings always** — liquidity first; monetize only sellers already succeeding (Jiji) |
| Assume card payments + escrow by default | Cash-on-meetup default; escrow introduced later as *optional paid trust product* (Carousell Protection, Vinted-style buyer fee) |
| Pure-online transactions (Carvana) | **Physical trust infrastructure**: inspection hubs, test-drive centers, agent-assisted deals (Cars24 hubs, Encar centers, Carousell Certified) |
| Desktop-era web + email leads | Mobile-only, chat-first (WhatsApp-grade in-app chat), phone-number identity |
| One vertical, deep | Horizontal first for traffic, then verticalize monetization into motors/property/jobs (OLX's explicit end-state) |
| Own inventory full-stack (Carvana, OLX Autos) | **Full-stack inventory mostly failed or bled cash in EMs** (OLX Autos exited entirely; Spinny still loss-making). Asset-light + certification layer beats owning cars |

### 5.3 Recommended feature set for the Sri Lankan platform

**Trust layer (the differentiator — steal from Encar, Vinted, Riyasewana):**
1. **Paid vehicle inspection + certification product** (Encar/Carousell Certified model): partner with garages initially, standardized 150+ point report, photo/video walkaround, platform badge, optional short powertrain warranty. Charge the seller (LKR-priced) or bundle into dealer packages. With 200%+ duties making every car a major asset, buyers will pay for certainty.
2. **DMT/CRIB-style verification integrations**: title check, outstanding-lease check (huge in SL where most vehicles carry leases), NIC-based seller verification. Riyasewana's DMT API shows this is doable.
3. **Escrow via LankaQR/CEFTS for smaller-ticket categories** (electronics, home & living): Vinted's model — free to sell, buyer pays ~4–6% + fixed fee "Buyer Protection," funds held until delivery confirmed. Position explicitly against Facebook Marketplace scams.
4. **Deal ratings** (CarGurus IMV): with import taxes making pricing opaque, a fair-value estimate per model-year + Great/Fair/Overpriced badges would be a genuine consumer magnet — and the pricing data becomes a B2B product (leasing companies, insurers, banks all need valuations).
5. **Ratings + verified badges + in-app chat** with off-platform-contact detection (standard C2C hygiene).

**Revenue architecture (phased):**
- **Phase 1 (liquidity)**: free listings everywhere; monetize via bumps/featured/urgent promos (Jiji/ikman model, mobile-money priced at LKR 300–2,000 tiers).
- **Phase 2 (B2B SaaS — the real money)**: dealer/agent/rental-fleet **subscription packages** with tiered visibility + tooling (AutoTrader/Rightmove model). Sri Lanka has thousands of car sales yards, brokers, and rent-a-car operators; ARPR-ladder economics (start ~LKR 10–50k/month) compound beautifully. Give agents analytics + lead management so the package is a workflow, not just ads.
- **Phase 3 (trust products)**: paid certification/inspection, buyer-protection escrow fees, extended warranty attach (Back Market: warranties/insurance already 18% of revenue).
- **Phase 4 (referral finance)**: **leasing/financing lead-gen** — in a 200%-duty market nearly every vehicle is leased; banks/leasing companies (LOLC, People's Leasing, etc.) will pay per funded lead. Same for property mortgages (PropertyGuru Finance) and vehicle insurance referrals. Consider Zillow-Flex-style *success-fee* real-estate referrals since SL brokers won't pay big upfront subscriptions initially.
