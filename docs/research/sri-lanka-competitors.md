# Sri Lankan Online Marketplace / Classifieds — Competitive Research Report

**Date:** 2026-09-01. **Method note:** Direct fetching of `.lk` domains was blocked by the research environment's network egress proxy, so findings come from web search results (which include indexed page content), news coverage, app-store listings, and third-party analytics. Items that could not be verified directly are flagged.

---

## 1. Platform Profiles

### 1.1 patpat.lk — general classifieds + embedded vehicle finance

- **What it is:** Founded 2017 as a vehicle classifieds site, evolved into a "hybrid classifieds and e-commerce ecosystem" / "digital lifestyle platform" ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/patpatlk/__SwVU-V-akHjXygbzEy26Fi2uqRLpXODs89Tn2iSiKts), [Sunday Observer](https://www.sundayobserver.lk/2022/06/05/business/patpatlk-turns-five)). Critically, the **patpat.lk app is developed by Citizens Development Business Finance PLC (CDB)**, a licensed Sri Lankan finance company ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/patpat-lk/id1385368825), [CDB partnership post](https://www.facebook.com/patpat.srilanka/posts/681449827354346/)) — patpat is effectively a finance-company-backed marketplace whose real engine is lead generation for leasing/loans.
- **Categories (5 verticals):** Vehicles (cars, SUVs, pickups, MPVs), Property (lands, houses, apartments, commercial, annexes/rooms, featured projects), Marketplace (electronics, home & living, pets, spare parts, fashion, health & beauty, agriculture, etc.), Education, Leisure — 100,000+ ads claimed ([patpat.lk about](https://patpat.lk/en/about), [Adaderana Biz](https://bizenglish.adaderana.lk/patpat-lk-is-now-sri-lankas-biggest-online-search-platform/)).
- **Listing/UX features:** Free posting for individuals and businesses; chat with sellers; saved favorites; alerts; **budget-based search** ("search by monthly installment" — see listings that match your monthly budget); each vehicle ad shows an **estimated lease rental** and an "APPLY FOR LEASE" button with an agent callback promised "within 3 minutes"; quick vehicle finance application form ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/patpat-lk/id1385368825), [FAQs](https://patpat.lk/en/frequently-asked-questions), [financial request page](https://patpat.lk/en/financial-request/vehicle/quick_apply)).
- **Financing:** Leasing, property loans, education loans, personal loans and credit cards brokered via CDB ([about page](https://patpat.lk/en/about)). This is the deepest financing integration of any SL classifieds site.
- **Auction feature:** Launched ~2021 — buyers bid on vehicles; patpat claimed 20,000-vehicle target inventory and 1,000 island-wide touchpoints ([Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/Motor/E-commerce-platform-patpat-lk-launches-revolutionary-Auction-feature/55-678047)). Current status unverified.
- **Vehicle inspection / price guide:** No evidence of an owned inspection service or published price index; blog content covers leasing guidance ([blog](https://patpat.lk/en/blog)). Unverified beyond that.
- **Monetization:** (1) **Premium Ad Services** (launched Aug 2022): *Premier Ad* (top of page, bigger, highlighted), *Boost Ad* (returns ad to top for the promotion period), *Quick Sell* (urgent-sale badge); durations 3–30 days; base posting stays free ([Adaderana Biz](https://bizenglish.adaderana.lk/patpat-lk-launches-premier-ad-services-to-give-more-value-addition-to-buyers-and-sellers/), [premiumads page](https://www.patpat.lk/premiumads)). **LKR prices not publicly indexed — could not verify.** (2) Finance lead-gen/commissions via CDB (implied by ownership; not disclosed). (3) Dealer/partner pages exist ([vehicle dealers page](https://www.patpat.lk/vehicle/dealers?page=1)) but package pricing is not published.
- **Traffic:** Claimed "Sri Lanka's biggest online search platform" in Sept 2021 (self-declared, contested — ikman clearly larger) ([Colombo Gazette](https://colombogazette.com/2021/09/22/patpat-lk-is-now-sri-lankas-biggest-online-search-platform/)). Similarweb lists patpat.lk as ikman's #2 most-similar site ([Similarweb competitors](https://www.similarweb.com/website/ikman.lk/competitors/)). Note: Similarweb's own category blurb miscategorizes patpat.lk as a clothing shop — almost certainly confusion with the unrelated US baby-clothing brand patpat.com; treat third-party "patpat" reviews with care (see §3).

### 1.2 autostream.lk — new-generation auto platform (inspection/data-led)

- **What it is:** An "all-in-one auto-mobility platform" for buying/selling new and used cars; officially launched **March 2026**, positioning itself explicitly against listing-fee marketplaces ([Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/business/auto-stream-is-on-a-mission-to-shape-the-future-of-car-buying-and-selling-in-sri-lanka), [autostream.lk](https://www.autostream.lk/)). Based in Wattala ([Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/www.autostream.lk/)). It is a marketplace + services platform, not an importer, though importers/auction agents (e.g., Nagoya Auto Auction, Lanka Auto Mart, Global Lanka) list on it via a **dealer directory** ([dealers list](https://www.autostream.lk/dealers-list/)).
- **Key features:**
  - **BLUE-T Grading** — a pre-purchase vehicle inspection/grading system "developed in collaboration with Japanese experts," 110+ checkpoints (engine, transmission, electrical, accident history, appearance, comfort), producing a condition grade to establish value ([BLUE-T page](https://www.autostream.lk/blue-t), [Grade My Car](https://autostream.lk/grade-my-car-mobile/)). This is the only marketplace-integrated inspection product found in the market.
  - **Valuation tool** and real-time price insights ([valuation](https://www.autostream.lk/valuation/)).
  - Financial calculators, auto news, model comparisons; EV/electrification content ([Newswire EV article](https://www.newswire.lk/business/auto-stream-says-sri-lanka-is-ready-to-adopt-electrification)).
- **Monetization:** 100% **free listings for both dealers and private sellers** ("eliminating the listing-fee barrier") ([Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/business/auto-stream-is-on-a-mission-to-shape-the-future-of-car-buying-and-selling-in-sri-lanka)). Revenue presumably from paid BLUE-T inspections/grading and valuation services; no published pricing found — **unverified**.
- **Traffic:** Too new for reliable third-party numbers; none found.

### 1.3 ikman.lk — the market leader (horizontal classifieds)

- **What it is:** Sri Lanka's largest classifieds site, launched June 2012, owned by Sweden-based **Saltside Technologies** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikman.lk)).
- **Categories:** 50+ — vehicles, electronics, property (sale/rent), jobs (ikmanJOBS, ~9,900 vacancies as of Aug 2026), services, home & garden, animals, etc. ([ikman.lk](https://ikman.lk/), [ikmanJOBS](https://ikman.lk/en/jobs)).
- **Features:** Trilingual **Sinhala / Tamil / English** — the first SL classifieds portal to offer all three ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikman.lk)); iOS/Android apps; chat; **Doorstep Delivery** e-commerce program with vetted sellers ([ikman blog](https://blog.ikman.lk/en/shop-easier-faster-and-safer-with-ikman-doorstep-delivery/)); **ikman Safe Buy** — escrow via European fintech Swiftcourt, money held until buyer confirms receipt, **buyer fee Rs 1,500** ([safe-buy page](https://ikman.lk/en/safe-buy)); **Authorised Dealer badge** for vehicle dealers ([blog](https://blog.ikman.lk/en/the-all-new-authorised-dealer-badge-exclusive-prominent-differentiated/)); **CarsGuide** (2023) — car review/specs/comparison content hub feeding dealer leads ([CarsGuide about](https://carsguide.ikman.lk/about-us/), [Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/2023/07/10/introducing-carsguide-sri-lankas-most-trusted-platform-for-car-reviews-news-tips-powered-by-ikman/)).
- **Monetization:** Freemium: free base ads (with per-category free-ad caps) + paid promotions — **Top Ad Rs 599**, **Bump Up Rs 200** (prices as announced; likely higher now — see §4), *Urgent* badge, promotion durations 3/7/15 days, bulk discounts 10–30% for businesses ([Adaderana Biz](https://bizenglish.adaderana.lk/ikman-lk-offers-options-to-attract-more-buyers-for-listed-advertisements/), [ikman blog](https://blog.ikman.lk/top-ad-bump-up-ads-now-come-with-more-and-better-options/)); tiered **memberships** for businesses (member page/shop, bonus promotions, dedicated account support) ([blog](https://blog.ikman.lk/en/ikman-lk-introduces-memberships-to-help-businesses-sell-more/), [Lanka Business News](https://www.lankabusinessnews.com/ikman-lk-differentiates-premier-memberships-with-attractive-value-additions/)); banner/display advertising; Safe Buy transaction fees.
- **Traffic:** ~**5.9M visits/month (March 2025, Similarweb data cited in a peer-reviewed comparison)** — highest of SL e-commerce sites, ahead of Daraz, with 7m02s average visit duration and 8.63 pages/visit ([ResearchGate study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391325762_A_Comparative_Analysis_of_Selected_Key_Performance_Indicators_of_Website_Engagement_Among_Seven_Leading_E-Commerce_Stores_in_Sri_Lanka)); ranked #38 globally in Similarweb's Classifieds category ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/ikman.lk/vs/riyasewana.com/)). Historically 3.3M unique monthly visitors in 2017 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikman.lk)).

### 1.4 riyasewana.com — the vehicle vertical incumbent

- **What it is:** Sri Lanka's largest dedicated vehicle marketplace, operating since **2009**; cars, vans, lorries, three-wheelers, motorbikes, buses, plus spare parts ([riyasewana.com](https://riyasewana.com/), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.riyasewana.android.riyasewana&hl=en)).
- **Features:** Powerful search filters incl. registered vs unregistered (recon) segmentation; ads live ~3 months; Bump Up and Top Ad promotions; chat/call contact; price comparisons and leasing offers from finance institutes advertised alongside listings ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.riyasewana.android.riyasewana&hl=en), [terms](https://riyasewana.com/terms.php)). Apps launched relatively late (iOS app 2021) ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/riyasewana/id1583270163)). English-first; no verified trilingual UI (unverified).
- **Monetization:** Free registered-vehicle ads; **Rs 650 non-refundable fee per unregistered/brand-new vehicle ad** (individuals and dealers alike); paid premium features (Top Ad/Bump Up); **Rs 5,000 fee to unblock a phone number** blocked for policy violations (e.g., advertising the leasing-installment figure as the price); banner ads ([riyasewana terms](https://riyasewana.com/terms.php)).
- **Traffic:** StatShow (low-reliability model) estimates ~1.48M monthly visitors / 3.25M pageviews ([StatShow](https://www.statshow.com/www/riyasewana.com)); Similarweb ranks it far below ikman overall (#7,284 in Classifieds) but names it ikman's **#1 competitor** by audience overlap ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/ikman.lk/competitors/)). Treat absolute numbers as rough.

### 1.5 lankapropertyweb.com — property vertical leader

- **What it is:** Established **2007**; Sri Lanka's most-visited property portal — houses, apartments, lands, commercial, rentals; 25,000+ ads claimed ([lankapropertyweb.com](https://www.lankapropertyweb.com/), [about](https://www.lankapropertyweb.com/aboutus.php)).
- **Features (notably data-rich):** **Sri Lanka House Price Index**, home-loan calculator and **home-loan comparison**, ROI calculator, area guides, buying guides, market-insights reports; runs the annual **Lanka Property Show** (physical + virtual) ([house prices](https://www.lankapropertyweb.com/house_prices.php), [market insights](https://www.lankapropertyweb.com/market-insights/)).
- **Monetization:** Free ads (90 days) + paid visibility upgrades; **agent subscription packages** (pricing not public — "contact us") ([FAQs](https://www.lankapropertyweb.com/FAQs.php), [subscribe page](https://www.lankapropertyweb.com/myaccount/subscribe.php)); event revenue; display ads.
- **Reputation:** Only 17 Trustpilot reviews, mixed; a notable complaint alleges ads get "dropped day by day" to pressure sellers into paying for visibility ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lankapropertyweb.com)).

### 1.6 Carmudi and other vehicle players

- **Carmudi Sri Lanka:** Rocket Internet's vehicle marketplace; its model charged **fees only to dealerships**, individuals listed free ([EconomyNext](https://economynext.com/carmudi-sri-lanka-upgrades-platform-330/)). It appears inactive in Sri Lanka today (carmudi.lk not among current ranked competitors), but **no source documenting a formal exit** was found — unverified; treat as effectively defunct locally.
- **AutoLanka.com:** "Sri Lanka's No.1 car website" claim; the country's first online automobile magazine + classifieds (cars, bikes, trucks, parts), free used-car listings ([autolanka.com](https://www.autolanka.com/)).
- **AutoMe (autome.lk), Careka.lk, carmarket.lk, Lankan Auto Market, MyCarLanka (app):** smaller free-listing car marketplaces; long tail, low differentiation ([autome.lk](https://autome.lk/), [careka.lk](https://careka.lk/en), [carmarket.lk](https://carmarket.lk/)).

### 1.7 Facebook Marketplace & Groups

- Facebook had **~9.0–10.4M users in Sri Lanka (≈47% of population)** in 2025 ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sri-lanka), [NapoleonCat](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/facebook-users-in-sri_lanka/2025/05/)). Marketplace has active SL vehicle sections and many large dedicated groups ("Used Cars for Sale Sri Lanka", "CAR MARKET – used car sales", "BUY AND SELL VEHICLES IN SRI LANKA") ([FB Marketplace Colombo cars](https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/108602292505393/cars/)).
- **Strengths:** zero cost, enormous reach, chat-native, Sinhala/Tamil used naturally by sellers.
- **Weaknesses:** no structured vehicle/property fields, no moderation of quality, and a globally documented scam problem (non-delivery, counterfeits, account hacking) ([ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/article/facebook-grew-marketplace-to-1-billion-users-now-scammers-are-using-it-to-target-people-around-the-world), [Comparitech](https://www.comparitech.com/identity-theft-protection/top-facebook-marketplace-scams/)). No SL-specific scam statistics found (unverified locally, but the mechanism is identical).

## 3. Gaps & Pain Points (prioritized, with evidence)

1. **Scams, fake listings and zero recourse — the #1 complaint.** ikman's Trustpilot sits around **1.5/5**; recurring themes: sellers vanishing after advance payments, misrepresented items (e.g., a 37-seater luxury bus that turned out to be an old 24-seater), unresolved fraud reports; ikman's stock response is that it "is a platform… not involved in transactions" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/ikman.lk), [Checksite](https://checksite.ai/report/ikman.lk)). Sri Lanka Police/CID list fake classified listings and advance-fee fraud among common online scams ([Newswire — 9 common scams](https://www.newswire.lk/2026/01/12/9-common-online-scams-listed-by-sri-lanka-police/)); documented classified-ad scam patterns include **vehicle-ownership fraud** (deposit taken on a car the seller doesn't own) and **fake escrow middlemen** ([findus.lk scam guide](https://findus.lk/blogs/classified-ad-scams-sri-lanka/)). ikman itself ran a public online-safety campaign in response to "rising scams" ([Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/2023/11/15/ikman-highlights-importance-of-online-safety-amidst-rising-scams/)).
2. **Escrow/payment protection exists but is marginal.** ikman Safe Buy (Swiftcourt escrow) is the only offering, costs the buyer Rs 1,500, and is buried as an opt-in for small goods — ikman simultaneously warns users *against* escrow sites generally ([safe-buy](https://ikman.lk/en/safe-buy), [stay-safe](https://ikman.lk/en/stay-safe)). Nothing equivalent for vehicles or property, the two highest-fraud-stakes categories. **Gap: trusted transaction rails for high-value items.**
3. **No verified sellers / identity verification as standard.** Verification exists only as paid dealer badges (ikman Authorised Dealer). Private-seller identity, ownership documents (CR book), and phone verification are not surfaced as trust signals anywhere. Riyasewana's model even monetizes bad behavior downstream (Rs 5,000 to unblock a number) rather than verifying upstream ([riyasewana terms](https://riyasewana.com/terms.php)).
4. **No vehicle history / inspection standard — only just emerging.** No Carfax-equivalent (import records, mileage verification, accident history) exists. Autostream's BLUE-T (110 checkpoints, launched 2026) is the first marketplace-integrated grading product ([BLUE-T](https://www.autostream.lk/blue-t)) — validating the gap, but it's new, car-only, and unproven at scale. ikman/patpat/riyasewana listings carry zero condition assurance.
5. **Weak price transparency.** No authoritative used-vehicle price guide (LPW's House Price Index is the only public index, in property). Riyasewana explicitly *warns/blocks* sellers for advertising the leasing-installment figure as the price — evidence of widespread deceptive pricing in listings ([terms](https://riyasewana.com/terms.php)). Autostream's "real-time price insights"/valuation is again the newcomer's answer to this gap.
6. **Pay-to-play visibility resentment.** Complaints that platforms delete/de-rank ads to force paid promotion: ikman ("deleting ads before expiration to collect more money," arbitrary account blocks — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/ikman.lk)) and LPW ("dropping ads day by day to get money to increase visibility" — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lankapropertyweb.com)). Autostream's entire go-to-market is "no listing fees," confirming fee fatigue as a perceived wedge ([Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/business/auto-stream-is-on-a-mission-to-shape-the-future-of-car-buying-and-selling-in-sri-lanka)).
7. **Poor customer service / ad management.** Trustpilot reviews cite inability to edit paid ads, unresponsive support, no refunds ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/ikman.lk?page=2)).
8. **Financing integration is fragmented.** patpat/CDB is the only deep integration (per-ad lease application, installment-based search); ikman and riyasewana offer only adjacent lead-gen; no cross-lender comparison for vehicles the way LPW compares home loans. A neutral multi-lender financing layer does not exist.
9. **Language gap.** Only ikman is genuinely trilingual (Sinhala/Tamil/English) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikman.lk)). Riyasewana, patpat, LPW, and autostream appear English-first — a real limitation given that vernacular users default to Facebook groups instead (could not fully verify patpat/riyasewana UI languages).
10. **Facebook leakage = unmet trust+free need.** Millions transact in unstructured FB groups despite scam exposure, because structured platforms either charge, cap free ads, or add friction. Any platform combining free listing + structure + trust verification attacks this leakage directly.

## 5. Caveats

- All `.lk` sites were unreachable directly from the research environment (egress-blocked), so per-page UX details (exact filter sets, current promo prices, language toggles on patpat/riyasewana) rest on indexed snippets and secondary coverage.
- "PatPat" review sites (ComplaintsBoard, Trustindex, Scam Detector's 5.9 score) largely conflate patpat.lk with the unrelated US clothing brand patpat.com — those were excluded from the gap analysis except as a caution.
- ikman membership prices are 2021-era; riyasewana traffic figures are model-based estimates (StatShow); autostream's March 2026 launch date and Carmudi's local exit are per single-source search summaries — verify before quoting externally.
- Reddit was inaccessible to the search crawler, so forum-level anecdotes come via Trustpilot, news coverage, and the findus.lk scam guide instead.
